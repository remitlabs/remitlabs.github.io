What is free, forever

If it makes your machine safer, it is free. The scanner, the local canary, the revocation feed, and the MCP load gate are not behind a paywall — ever. Paying for a kill switch would make the ecosystem less safe, and we are not doing that.

Paid tiers sell coordination: a second person can see the fleet, approve an install, and prove what happened. That is a different job from scanning one machine.

Pricing questions

Is the local audit really free?
Yes. No account, no seat count, no time limit. Run npx @remitlabs/cli@latest start and use it.
What counts as an operator?
Anyone who can change policy, approve an install, run a hosted canary, or administer the org. Read-only viewers are unlimited and free, so a security team can share the fleet without a 50-seat bill.
Why per operator, not per skill?
Value scales with the number of people who can see and act on the fleet, not with the number of files. Per-skill pricing punishes exactly the teams that need this most.
Do you store my code?
The local scan runs on your machine. The self-hosted API is yours. The hosted team tier will document exactly what leaves the machine before it ships — see the security model.
Is this a guarantee that a skill is safe?
No. Remit is defense in depth: static findings, one observed canary run, digest pinning, and revocation. Every receipt states its coverage and limits.
Can I self-host?
Yes, on Enterprise. The API already runs on Postgres/PGlite; packaging, docs, backups, and an operator credential are part of the Enterprise deployment.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
Export everything (JSON/markdown) for 30 days, then delete on request. No lock-in on your own evidence.
Do you offer a discount?
20% off annual. Design partners get 50% off for six months in exchange for feedback. Open-source projects and students: ask.

Bring us one real fleet.

We will bring the audit. Design partners get the Team tier at 50% off for six months, a direct line to the founder, and a say in what gets built first. In exchange: a 30-minute kickoff, a check-in every two weeks, and permission to use anonymized learnings.
