Pricing questions

Is the local audit really free?
Yes. Run npx @remitlabs/cli@latest start to scan your installed skills and open a local report. The local plan has no account requirement or time limit.
What counts as an operator?
In the proposed plans, an operator can change policy, approve an install, run a hosted test or manage the organization. Read-only viewers are unlimited and free.
How is the planned team price calculated?
The proposed price depends on the people who can manage the service. Each paid plan also has a minimum bill, shown above. The number of skill files does not affect that price.
Do you store my code?
The local scan keeps skill content on your computer. For the planned hosted service, the data flow will be published before release. See the data-handling details.
Is this a guarantee that a skill is safe?
A scan can miss harmful behavior. Container tests show what happened during one run. Read the findings and missing checks before approving a skill.
Can I self-host?
Self-hosting is planned for Enterprise. The API runs on Postgres or PGlite. Enterprise packaging, documentation, backups and operator access are part of the proposed plan.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
The proposed hosted-service terms provide JSON or Markdown exports for 30 days after cancellation, followed by deletion on request.
Do you offer a discount?
The proposed discounts are 20% for annual billing and 50% for design partners for six months. Email to discuss open-source or student pricing.

Help shape the team service.

As a design partner, you can discuss your needs directly with the founder and help prioritize the planned service. The proposed Team discount is 50% for six months. Participation includes a 30-minute kickoff, check-ins every two weeks and permission to use anonymized feedback.
