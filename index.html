Check a skill before trusting it.

A skill gives your AI agent instructions and may include code to run. It can ask the agent to read private files, send data online or change your settings.

Remit shows you the files and patterns worth reviewing. You choose which skills to keep.

Scan your installed skills

The scan reads SKILL.md and its scripts. It checks 64 known risk patterns and lists the findings in a local report.

Test how a skill behaves

With Docker or Podman, you can also test a skill in a temporary container. The report shows the activity observed and which checks were missed. The test runs without your agent or model.

Understand the limits before you start.

The scan runs on your computer. Its file checks work offline once Remit is installed. The source code is available under the Apache-2.0 license.

A scan can miss harmful code, including compiled or encrypted files. A test shows what happened during one run. Network blocking during a test requires an explicit list of allowed destinations.

Use the report to decide what to inspect, keep or remove.
