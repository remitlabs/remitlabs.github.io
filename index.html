A skill is a document, a program, and a permission slip.

A description tells you what a skill claims to do. The instructions and scripts beside it determine what your agent may actually do with your files, keys and network access.

Remit gives you evidence before you grant that authority, and controls for the versions you keep.

With Docker or Podman available, run a canary in a disposable container. Its receipt records observed behavior, the rules that fired, and any missing coverage.
