The honest answers

you have socket or snyk

Keep them. They cover dependencies and code, and Snyk's ToxicSkills research is why teams are paying attention. Run the free Remit audit next to them and compare what each one sees about the skill artifact: declared scope, observed behavior, digest history, and revocation. A scan report is not an install gate or a kill switch.

you use skills.sh or clawhub

Keep using them for discovery. Install counts are popularity, not evidence. In August 2026 a typosquatting campaign on skills.sh reached 1.7 million installs before it was pulled (Zenity). Unit 42 crawled all 49,943 OpenClaw skills and found 80.0% did something their own description did not mention (Unit 42, June 2026). Remit is the trust layer under the registry, not a replacement for it. A registry with a receipt is more valuable to everyone.

you rely on platform signing

That is necessary and not sufficient. A team running three agents still needs one neutral policy and one audit answer across all three. Vendors will not build a competitor-neutral control plane. That is the job Remit does.

you read every skill by hand

That is the most thorough control available, and it does not scale past 50 skills or survive a maintainer changing one at 2am. Remit automates the read, records what it saw, and tells you when the bytes change. It does not replace judgment; it gives judgment a memory.

you block all skills

Then you do not have the productivity problem yet. When you allow them, start with the free audit. No pitch needed.

What Remit does not claim

Not a guarantee

A finding is a rule match. A canary is one observed run. A clean receipt is not proof that a skill is benign.

Not a firewall

Runtime guardrails for prompts and models are a different layer. Remit governs the artifact the agent loads.

Not a registry

Discovery is solved by better-funded products. Remit adds evidence and lifecycle to whatever registry you already use.

Not finished

The local tool ships today. The team control plane is in design-partner development, and I label it that way.

Run the free audit and judge the evidence.
